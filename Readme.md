# Teardowns of everyday software products that I use

This repo is my attempt at starting something new in my journey of software engineering. An attempt to craft or should I say reignite my passion for softwares that 
I somewhere lost during the Data structures and leetcode rush.

Here I will take a product that I use everyday, but I know very less about it. And try to research few pre-thought questions and find their
answers.

Here are the basic questions that I will start with. They are minimum. As interest grows I will eventually write more.

# Product Name

## 1. What problem does it solve?
<!-- What pain, friction, or unmet need exists? -->

## 2. Who has this problem?
<!-- Who is the specific user/customer? -->

## 3. How was this problem solved before?
<!-- What did people do before this product existed? -->

## 4. Why is this solution better?
<!-- Why would someone switch from the old way or a competitor? -->

## 5. What is the core insight?
<!-- What did the founders understand that made this product possible? -->

## 6. How did it get its first 100 users?
<!-- Where did the initial users come from? What distribution channel or strategy likely worked? -->

## 7. How does it make money?
<!-- Who pays, what do they pay for, and why is it worth paying for? -->

## 8. What would I build differently?
<!-- If I were building this today, what would I change or improve? -->

## 9. What surprised me?
<!-- Something I didn't understand about the product/problem before investigating it. -->
