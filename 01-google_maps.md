# Google Maps

## 1. What problem does it solve?
1- How to go from place a to b?
2- Route discovery.
3- ETA for the route.
4- Best possible timing/route.
5- Place discovery.

## 2. Who has this problem?
Before google maps, people had all the above problems.
Google solved all these problems and further.

## 3. How was this problem solved before?
1- Paper maps
2- Asking people for advice.
3- Local knowledge.
4- Dedicated GPS devices.
5- Contacting buisness or restuarants.

## 4. Why is this solution better?
Because it reduced lengthy workflows to simple search and discovery. People also got to know their locations in real time and route discovery and ETA solutions all in one.

## 5. What is the core insight?
Reduces time wasted for a simple work. Also reduces cognitive load of learning a new skill like map reading and calculation.

## 6. How did it get its first 100 users?
Google search already had traffic. Maps became and extension to search a place. Intial feature was search a place, get directions from a to b. drag and zoom.

## 7. How does it make money?
 Buisnesses pay to appear first in the searches. Basically advertisement.

 ## 8. What would I build differently?
 Probably parking and travelling conviniences can be increased, like help me find if there are parking spaces.

 ## 9. What surprised me?
 What surprised me was google maps in one of the first implementations of AJAX, asynchronous javascript and xml. Here a page doesnt need to reload to load missing information.
