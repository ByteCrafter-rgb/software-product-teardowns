# Whatsapp

## 1. What problem does it solve?
On their About page, whatsapp says: "We started as an alternative to SMS". It had few distinct features such as, showing status of people, adding all your contacts into the app.

When in June 2009, apple launched push notifications , whatsapp in-corporated it so that people can see their contacts statuses when they change it. It gained traction as it was released just one month after whatsapp's launch.

## 2. Who has this problem?
SMS usually costed money and other messaging apps like facebook were not primary messaging apps. So users did not have a specific messenger app with more easy to use and less expensive than SMS. Also later additional features like VoIP and video calls were revolutionary features. Also group chats too.

## 3. How was this problem solved before?
Primarily SMS and email, or social media apps like Facebook etc. But facebook wanted users to have a facebook account and in no way everyone who has a mobile phone has a facebook account. Specially the older generation at that time.

## 4. Why is this solution better?
This solution is better because it worked as an upgraded SMS feature, which used simple and ordinary features like phone and phone numbers and a simple internet connection for it to work, no email sign up etc.

## 5. What is the core insight?
Whatsapp won because it removed friction between two people communicating, if you have a phone and internet connection, you are all set.

## 6. How did it get its first 100 users?
Its first users were russian freinds of one of the first enginners. When apple launched push notifications, users started to communicate around the status updates, then foudners realised that they can use it as messagin focused app.

## 7. How does it make money?
Buisnesses pay to use whatsapp buisness features. Also Meta runs ads for whatsapp direct messages.

## 8. What would I build differently?
I would like to segregate the personal and buisness comm on whatsapp and also would like to add a buisness discovery feature where I would like to know registered buisness owners contacts so that I can contact them directly

## 9. What surprised me?
That whatsapp was not even a messaging app initally. It was just a status update app, where people would update what they were doing currently and whether they are available to call or not.
